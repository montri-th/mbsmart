# smart pylon artwork study

The symbol and five wordmark paths are copied verbatim from the [official smart UK model page](https://uk.smart.com/en/models/hashtag-five/), retrieved 13 September 2026. White fill follows the supplied pylon reference. No generic font or redrawn symbol is used.

The 70 × 95 stacked wrapper and 12-unit separation are a study composition, not a supplied complete fabrication lockup. Final spacing, illumination, lettering and supplier production artwork require smart approval. The old reconstructed triangle/circle mark and second large lower smart wordmark were removed.

smart trademarks remain the rights holder's. Public availability is not an open licence or an approval of this proposal. Assets are included solely to identify the brand in this owner-requested design review. Private manual pages and pricing are not included.
